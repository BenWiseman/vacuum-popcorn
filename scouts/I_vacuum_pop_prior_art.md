# Scout I: prior art for "tiny temporary vacuum pops" (2026-10-01)

**Summary.** NOT FOUND: any paper proposing collapse of small false-vacuum pockets, bags or domain walls as a present-day source of cosmic rays above 1e20 eV. Searches: INSPIRE abstract and title queries, web searches, and a keyword scan of all 108 INSPIRE records citing the Amaterasu paper (2311.14231). NOT FOUND means not found by these. The parts exist separately in early-universe and heavy-ion work: collapse with Lorentz-contracted walls (hep-ph/0105272), reflection energy gain on a shrinking false-vacuum remnant (2206.09923), N-body pockets with reflecting walls (2407.01676, 2305.07702). The last two give limits: trapped particles halt or reverse the collapse, and a mass-step wall reflects only below a wall-frame momentum threshold. Closest present-day claims: 2505.15764 (relativistic walls as a cosmic-ray source) and 2609.24714 (stable false-vacuum pockets holding axions up to 0.4 EeV). The defect literature dismissed domain walls for overclosure and lost its models to photon limits (1612.01517). No hit for the item 5 phrases. "(mine)" marks my inference.

## 1. Collapsing bubbles, walls, bags as sources
- **hep-ph/0105272 (Ray, Sanyal, Srivastava; NPA 712, 329).** Heavy ions, no cosmic rays. "This bubble undergoes relativistic collapse, with highly Lorentz contracted bubble walls, and may concentrate the entire energy into extremely small regions. Eventually different portions of bubble wall collide, with the energy being released in the form of particle production." RHIC parameters: γ "about 500 to 4000" at R = 1 fm, "5 × 10^4 to 3 × 10^5" at 0.1 fm, roughly 1/R² (mine).
- **Kazanas et al., PLB 142, 23 (1984; INSPIRE 206707).** "bubbles of a metastable superdense state of nuclear matter, created in high energy (E ∼ 10^15 eV) cosmic ray collisions": products of cosmic rays, not sources.
- **2501.15848.** Expanding true-vacuum bubbles today: "a macroscopically large burst of high energy neutrinos and photons from Higgs decays". The wall expands toward us.
- Bubble-wall collisions as colliders are early-universe only (2412.18752: "energy reach close to the Planck scale").

## 2. Top-down defect literature
- **Hill, Schramm, Walker (FERMILAB-Pub-86/147-T; PRD 36, 1007).** My grep of the preprint finds no "wall", "domain", "bubble" or "collapse". "Above this energy fermions cease to be trapped on the string and will be ejected into the vacuum." Abstract: "Cosmic-ray physics places significant limits on these scenarios."
- **astro-ph/9811011 (Bhattacharjee, Sigl).** No domain-wall section. Walls appear once: "the recognition of the disastrous role of massive domain walls in cosmology". Cores are "a region of unbroken symmetry ("false vacuum") surrounded by broken symmetry regions ("true vacuum")". Bubbles appear once, for relic X production: "creation in bubble wall collisons if inflation is completed by a first order phase transition".
- **Walls ruled out.** astro-ph/0103152: "Domain walls are ruled out because they contribute too much total mass to the universe." astro-ph/0006111: "domain walls, if they were formed at a scale that could explain EHECR, would over-close the Universe." These address stable early-universe walls; a pocket that forms and collapses today is not covered (mine).
- **Necklaces, vortons, monopolonium, cusps** (astro-ph/9803271, hep-ph/9701246, astro-ph/9412053, Bhattacharjee PRD 40, 3968): by their abstracts and the review's sections, none treats walls or bubbles.
- **What killed them.** astro-ph/9803271: "All these sources have an excess of pions (and thus photons) over nucleons at production." astro-ph/9811011: the model "is severely constrained by existing measurements and limits on the diffuse gamma ray background at energies below 100 GeV". 1612.01517 (Auger, v3): "The new results rule-out the early top-down models ... in which ultra-high energy cosmic rays are produced by, e.g., the decay of super-massive particles ... and challenge the most recent super-heavy dark matter models"; photon fraction below 0.14% above 1 EeV, 2.9% above 10 EeV. A burst into QCD jets would face the same limits (mine).

## 3. Relativistic walls and reflection
- **2010.02590, 2101.05721, 2007.08440.** None discusses cosmic rays in its text (grep). 2010.02590 Eq. 44, for wall length L ∼ 1/⟨φ⟩: "M < Mmax = √(γmax Tnuc ⟨φ⟩)".
- **2206.09923 (Kawana, Lu, Xie), Sec. 2.2.** "Consider a spherical shrinking FV remnant." Energy gain per reflection "δE = Ẽ − E = 2 v_w (v_w E + p_z)/(1 − v_w²)" (Eq. 2.9), "δE → ∞ for v_w → 1 as expected, assuming the particles remain trapped." For p_z = E: Ẽ = E(1+v)/(1−v) ≈ 4γ²E (mine).
- **2407.01676 (Carenza et al.).** "N-body simulations with a finite number of ultrarelativistic axion particles subject to reflective boundary conditions at the wall"; "Shortly before the wall would collapse, the front 'passes through the origin' and strikes the contracting dilaton wall, causing it to bounce back with a high γw factor."
- **2305.07702 (Lewicki et al.).** "the backreaction of the particles on the bubble slows or even reverses the collapse." "At the minimal radius ... the particles carry almost all of the energy". "R → 0 can be realized only if all particles eventually escape the bubble and the final stages of the collapse proceed as they would in vacuum." Eq. 2: full reflection only for "u² < m_j² − m_i²", u being the wall-frame normal momentum. 2212.10572: "Once γ becomes ≫ (Lm)^-1, reflection probabilities for all polarizations die off exponentially".
- **Consequence (mine, from Eq. 2).** With Δm = √(m_j² − m_i²), reflection needs u ≈ 2γE below Δm, so a reflected particle leaves with at most about 2γΔm. Repeated 4γ² gains need a wall that reflects at any energy. For a confining wall, 2007.08440 gives ejected quark energy "Eej,p ≃ γwp f" (Eq. 32), linear in γ. A bag wall treated as a perfect mirror: NOT FOUND.
- **Cosmic rays today: 2505.15764 (Yin).** "Their large momentum transfer can boost Standard-Model or hidden particles throughout cosmic space, effectively making the walls a source of cosmic rays. For a typical injected momentum pX ∼ mϕ γw the flux is estimated as" "≃ 10 km^-2 yr^-1" (Eq. 23), with mϕ ≲ 1 MeV (Eq. 5). One cosmic wall crossing matter; no energy above 1e20 eV stated.

## 4. Quark nuggets, strangelets, bags
- **astro-ph/0211597 (Madsen, Larsen; PRL 90, 121102).** "The high charge allows astrophysical strangelet acceleration to energies orders of magnitude higher than for protons."
- **astro-ph/0301575 (Ouyed, Keränen).** "a neutron star experiences an explosive phase transition into a quark star"; "E > 10^18.6 eV" via shock acceleration, not reflection.
- **Witten (PRD 30, 272)** derives nuggets from a first-order QCD transition; Alcock, Farhi (PRD 32, 1273): early-Universe evaporation. 1908.02501: "most of the matter ejected during and after the merger of two strange quark stars evaporates into nucleons".
- NOT FOUND: collapsing or evaporating nuggets or bags as a late-time UHECR source.

## 5. Phrases
- "vacuum pop", "vacuum popcorn": NOT FOUND. "transient vacuum": only unrelated hits. "false vacuum pockets": 2401.14331 ("rare false vacuum pockets of super-Hubble size collapse to PBHs"), early universe, no cosmic rays.
