## Banned

- Transactional words for ideas or quantities: cost, costs, buy, buys, pay, pays, owe, owes,
  budget, price, cheap, expensive, worth, spend, earn, afford, bargain, trade-off.
- Stock kitchen phrases: recipe for disaster, food for thought, half-baked, proof of the pudding,
  boils down to, cook up, simmer, back burner, spill the beans, cherry-pick, sweet spot, icing on
  the cake, bread and butter, piece of cake, melting pot, a taste of, whet the appetite.
- Words that clash with physics jargon: flavour (a neutrino and quark property), kernel (a
  mathematical term), "popcorn mechanism" (a hadronisation term).
- The benlm list: em dashes, contrastive "it isn't X, it's Y", enumerating previews,
  fragments for drama, present-participle openers, "a read", "a tell", "the through-line".
